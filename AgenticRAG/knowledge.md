**Executive Summary Report**

**Introduction:**
This report provides an analysis of the top recurring issues, average resolution times, and sentiment insights to identify bottlenecks and suggest actionable improvements. The goal is to enhance the overall customer experience, reduce frustration, and increase efficiency in resolving issues.

**Top Recurring Issues:**
The top three recurring issues are:

1. **Login Issues**: 50 tickets, with an average resolution time of 48 hours.
2. **Billing Discrepancies**: 30 tickets, with an average resolution time of 12 hours.
3. **Feature Requests**: 20 tickets, with many closed tickets without resolution.

**Sentiment Insights:**
The sentiment insights reveal:

1. Growing frustration with login issue delays.
2. Confusion with password reset UI.
3. Difficulty with account verification process.

**Bottlenecks:**
The analysis has identified the following bottlenecks:

1. **Login Issues**: The high volume of login issue tickets and long average resolution time indicate a significant bottleneck.
2. **Billing Discrepancies**: The volume of billing discrepancy tickets presents a bottleneck, despite relatively short average resolution times.
3. **Feature Requests**: The high number of closed tickets without resolution suggests a bottleneck in the feature request process.
